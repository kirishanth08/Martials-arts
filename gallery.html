// 04 • TOURNAMENT DIARY

THE ENERGY BEHIND THE PODIUM

Competitive success is never an accident. For every medal hanging in our trophy display, there are hundreds of early morning sprints, tens of thousands of repeated kata turns, and bruised shins conditioned on the tatami.

Our travel competition team represents the academy across state, national, and international stages. Students as young as eight compete alongside adult master practitioners, bound by mutual support.
