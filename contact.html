// 05 • FIRST DAY WALKTHROUGH

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU ARRIVE

We know walking into a martial arts dojo for the first time can feel intimidating. Here is exactly what your orientation looks like.

01
Check In at Reception

Show your digital pass code. You will be greeted by an assistant sensei who gives you a quick tour of locker facilities.

02
Receive Loaner Uniform

We provide a freshly laundered white uniform and belt. If you prefer athletic sweatpants and a t-shirt, that is completely fine too.

03
10-Minute Stance Briefing

Before group class begins, an instructor walks you through the bow, the basic guard, and the first forward stance on the side mat.

04
Join the Class

You participate at your own physical pace. Zero sparring or dangerous contact during trial visits—guaranteed.
