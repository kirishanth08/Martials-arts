// 02 • BIOGRAPHY & LINEAGE

AN UNBROKEN CHAIN OF TRADITION

Master Arjun began his martial arts journey at age seven under the direct tutelage of Sensei Hiroshi Taniguchi in Osaka, Japan. For six grueling years, his training consisted of dawn kihon repetitions, traditional makiwara striking conditioning, and deep immersion in Shotokan philosophy.

Upon returning home, he competed on the international World Karate Federation (WKF) circuit, capturing gold medals in individual kumite (2014, 2017) and silver in team kata. In 2012, he founded KAI Martial Arts Academy to preserve authentic Japanese standards without commercial compromises.

"Lineage is not about boasting about your teachers' names; it is about carrying the flame of their technical standard without dropping an ounce of quality."
Lineage Credentials
  • 1998 - 2004 Apprentice under Sensei Hiroshi Taniguchi (8th Dan, Osaka)
  • 2008 Conferred 3rd Dan Sandan at Tokyo JKA Hombu Dojo
  • 2016 Conferred 4th Dan Yondan & Appointed National Referee
