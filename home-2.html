// CURRICULUM BLUEPRINT

The Student Progression Roadmap

Our curriculum removes guesswork. Every stage of your martial arts journey is mapped with clear benchmarks, grading criteria, and measurable physical adaptations.

MONTHS 1 – 3
Phase 1: Foundation Kinetics

Mastering foundational stance stability (Zenkutsu-dachi), diaphragmatic breathing, core striking mechanics, and safe break-falls.

  • Basic straight punch (Choku-zuki)
  • Front snap kick (Mae-geri)
  • White to Yellow Belt exam
MONTHS 4 – 6
Phase 2: Combinations & Speed

Transitioning single techniques into fluid combinations. Introduction to formal kata routines, pad work rhythm, and evasion footwork.

  • Kata Heian Shodan mastery
  • Side thrust kick (Yoko-geri)
  • Yellow to Orange Belt exam
MONTHS 7 – 12
Phase 3: Tactical Sparring

Controlled partner sparring (Kumite). Distance management, counter-attacks, defensive angle cutting, and intermediate forms.

  • Controlled point sparring
  • Green & Blue belt testing
  • Joint lock defense basics
MONTHS 12+
Phase 4: Dan Mastery Track

Pre-Black Belt and Dan candidate preparation. Practical self-defense application (Bunkai), teaching assistantship, and competition.

  • Brown to Black Belt syllabus
  • Senior kata interpretation
  • Junior coaching mentorship
