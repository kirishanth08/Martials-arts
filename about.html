// 02 • THE ACADEMY STORY

FROM A HUMBLE 800 SQ FT MAT TO AN INTERNATIONAL TRAINING CENTER

KAI began in a modest warehouse space with twelve dedicated practitioners who wanted more than casual fitness routines. Head Master Arjun Rao, having competed at the highest levels in Asia and Europe, recognized that modern commercial martial arts had watered down traditional standards.

Over twelve years, our dojo expanded to a state-of-the-art 6,500 square foot combative facility while keeping our founder's solemn promise: every belt awarded must be tested in real sparring, verified by a board of black belts, and earned through blood, sweat, and humility.

// 03 • THE CODE

THE 5 DOJO VIRTUES (DOJO KUN)

Recited before and after every training session, these five principles govern the conduct of every KAI student both on the tatami and out in the world.

VIRTUE 01

SEEK PERFECTION OF CHARACTER

Martial skill without moral grounding creates bullies. Our training prioritizes emotional regulation, humility, and being a dependable anchor for family and community.

VIRTUE 02

BE FAITHFUL & LOYAL

Uphold honesty with yourself and dedication to your training partners. Mutual trust is what allows two students to strike with full speed while keeping each other completely safe.

VIRTUE 03

ENDEAVOR & PERSEVERANCE

Enduring discomfort builds an indomitable spirit. What you overcome on the mat teaches you that defeat is merely an informative pause before your next adjustment.

VIRTUE 04

RESPECT OTHERS & ETICQUETTE

Every session begins with a bow to the founder, instructors, and peers. True strength carries no arrogance; it bows first and speaks with quiet confidence.

VIRTUE 05

REFRAIN FROM VIOLENT BEHAVIOR

We train to fight so that we never have to. The consummate martial artist de-escalates conflict, defends the vulnerable, and uses force strictly as an unavoidable last resort.

// 04 • FACILITY STANDARDS

ENGINEERED FOR ELITE ATHLETIC SAFETY

Training intensity requires zero compromise on hygiene and impact protection. Our dojo features Olympic-grade shock-absorption underflooring to protect knees and spine during high-impact throws and kicks.

Olympic Sanctioned Tatami Flooring

50mm closed-cell high-density impact foam with anti-microbial textured vinyl surface.

Custom Heavy Bag & Pad Racks

12 heavy leather bags ranging from 80lb speed bags to 150lb banana bags for low-kick endurance.

Athlete Recovery & Changing Suites

Full locker rooms, rainfall showers, ice recovery tubs, and foam roller mobility station.
