// 02 • DISCIPLINE CURRICULUM

ABOUT THE SHOTOKAN CURRICULUM

Shotokan Karate is characterized by its deep, grounded stances and powerful direct hip transitions. Under JKA-certified instructors, students progress systematically through core technical sequences.

Founded on the principle of "Ikken Hissatsu" (to defeat with one blow), our curriculum emphasizes uncompromising technical precision before explosive acceleration.

PHASE 01

Kihon & Stance Biomechanics

Mastery of forward stance (Zenkutsu-dachi), horse-riding stance (Kiba-dachi), reverse punches (Gyaku-zuki), and three-point blocking mechanics.

PHASE 02

Kata Architecture & Bunkai

In-depth study of standard Heian katas (Shodan through Godan), Tekki Shodan, and Bassai Dai, including practical applications against realistic grabs.

PHASE 03

Yakusoku & Jiyu Kumite

Progression from pre-arranged five-step sparring (Gohon Kumite) into dynamic freestyle continuous sparring with protective gear and timing drills.

PHASE 04

Mokuso & Dojo Kun Philosophy

Integration of breath regulation, mental fortitude, humility, and the 5 precepts of Gichin Funakoshi into everyday moral character.

// 03 • ATHLETIC ADAPTATIONS

WHAT YOU DEVELOP ON THE MAT

Full-body physical and mental architecture. Over 6 to 12 months of consistent training, our students develop measurable improvements in neurological reflex speed, core rotational power, and mental composure.

  • Devastating kinetic strike generation through rotational hip torquing
  • Rock-solid physical stability, spatial awareness, and posture alignment
  • Reflexive blocking instincts against sudden linear and hook attacks
  • Mental discipline, reduced stress reactivity, and razor-sharp focus
