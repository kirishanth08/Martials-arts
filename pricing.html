// 05 • TRANSPARENCY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Straightforward answers regarding our billing terms, testing fees, and cancellation policy.

Are there long-term contracts?

No. All tuition is billed on a month-to-month basis. You can pause or cancel your enrollment at any time with 14 days written notice prior to your next billing cycle.

What does belt testing cost?

Kyu rank testing (White through Brown) costs a nominal $35 testing fee to cover examiner grading time, official belt, and printed certificate. Elite members have testing included.

Can I freeze membership for vacation or injury?

Yes. Students can freeze their account for up to 60 days per year for travel, academic exams, or medical recovery with zero pause fees.

What happens during my first class?

You'll arrive 15 minutes before class. We provide a loaner uniform. An assistant instructor will guide you through basic stances before you join the group warmup.
