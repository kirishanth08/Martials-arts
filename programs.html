// 03 • STUDENT PLACEMENT

FINDING YOUR LEVEL ON THE MAT

We never throw beginners into high-intensity sparring. Every student progresses through an organized, safe 3-tier placement pathway.

Request Skills Assessment
TIER 1 • 0 TO 6 MONTHS

FOUNDATION & ETIQUETTE

Focus is 100% on stance structure, basic striking mechanics, safe falling techniques (ukemi), and dojo etiquette.

Sparring: No live sparring • Non-contact pad drills only
TIER 2 • 6 TO 24 MONTHS

INTERMEDIATE TACTICS

Introduction of timing combinations, distance reading, defense counters, and controlled light-touch contact sparring with headgear.

Sparring: Light touch point kumite • Controlled partner drills
TIER 3 • 2+ YEARS

ADVANCED & COMPETITIVE

Tournament preparation, 3-minute continuous rounds, advanced bunkai kata analysis, and national federation testing.

Sparring: Full tournament rules • Dan exam grading
// 04 • TRAINING ANATOMY

THE STRUCTURE OF A 60-MINUTE SESSION

Every class follows a strict military-style time protocol designed to warm up cold joints safely, drill muscle memory repetitions, and conclude with mental centering.

10m
Warmup & Dynamic Joint Mobility
Cardio elevation, hip openers, wrist/neck conditioning, light calisthenics.
20m
Kihon Fundamentals & Technical Drilling
Linear combinations, pad strikes, target accuracy repetitions, stance balance.
20m
Kata Application or Controlled Kumite
Applying movements against realistic pressure, footwork angles, partner drills.
10m
Mokuso (Meditation) & Mat Bow Out
Breathing regulation, heart-rate recovery, dojo kun recital, formal bow to sensei.
// 05 • MAT DISCIPLINE

UNIFORM STANDARDS & DOJO PROTOCOL

The white gi (uniform) symbolizes equality. In the dojo, social status, profession, and age take second place to dedication and mutual respect.

Starter Gear Packages
Clean Uniform (Dogi)

Uniforms must be washed after every session, pressed, and worn with the KAI crest patch on the left chest.

Bowing at the Threshold

Students bow when stepping onto or off the tatami to show respect for the training space and their fellows.

Mandatory Protective Gear

Mouthguards, groin protectors, and sanctioned shin/instep guards are mandatory for all intermediate sparring classes.
