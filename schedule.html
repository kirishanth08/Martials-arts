// 04 • CLASS PROTOCOL

PREPARING FOR YOUR CLASS

Follow these standard dojo guidelines to maximize your safety and preserve tatami cleanliness.

Arrive 15 Minutes Early

Change into your gi, warm up cold joints on the side mat, and complete your attendance check-in before the bell.

Hydration & Nutrition

Bring a sealed water bottle. Avoid heavy meals within 90 minutes of class to prevent cramping during high-intensity kumite.

Jewelry & Nails Protocol

All rings, necklaces, and watches must be removed. Finger and toenails must be trimmed short to protect partners.
