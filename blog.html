// 05 • MARTIAL SCHOLARSHIP

ESSENTIAL MARTIAL ARTS LITERATURE

Recommended foundational texts kept in the KAI library for serious practitioners.

CLASSIC TEXT
Karate-Do: My Way of Life
By Gichin Funakoshi

The essential autobiography of the founder of Shotokan, detailing his training in Okinawa and the moral code of the empty hand.

STRATEGY
The Book of Five Rings (Go Rin No Sho)
By Miyamoto Musashi

The seminal 17th-century treatise on sword strategy, timing, psychological warfare, and seizing instantaneous advantage.

TACTICS
Meditations on Violence
By Rory Miller

A critical, unromantic examination of how real-world interpersonal violence operates compared to stylized dojo drills.
