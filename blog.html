// 04 • MARTIAL SCHOLARSHIP

ESSENTIAL MARTIAL ARTS LITERATURE

Foundational texts and strategic treatises kept in the KAI study hall for dedicated students.

CLASSIC TEXT
Karate-Do: My Way of Life
By Gichin Funakoshi

The essential autobiography of the father of modern Shotokan, detailing his training in Okinawa and the moral compass of the empty hand.

STRATEGY
The Book of Five Rings (Go Rin No Sho)
By Miyamoto Musashi

The seminal 17th-century treatise on duel strategy, spatial timing, psychological pressure, and seizing instantaneous initiative.

TACTICS
Meditations on Violence
By Rory Miller

A critical, unsentimental examination of real-world interpersonal conflict dynamics versus ritualized dojo sparring patterns.
